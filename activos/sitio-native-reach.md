# acme-multilingual.example — What is Native Reach™

> Fuente: https://www.acme-multilingual.example/native-reach/ · extraído 2026-09-05

## Definición (textual)

> Native Reach™ es "a new category of marketing focused on creating, adapting,
> distributing, and measuring content that performs **InSpeech, InContext, and InRegion.**"

> Es "the **multilingual evolution of Inbound Marketing**, purpose-built for brands that operate
> across markets and languages."

## Los tres pilares (textual)

1. **InSpeech** — "Every sentence should read as if it had been written locally."
2. **InContext** — "Messaging should honour local values, humour and context."
3. **InRegion** — "Campaigns should match local demand, local rivals, and local buyer needs."

## El framework de cuatro etapas

Opera como un ciclo continuo:

1. **Content Intelligence** (Reach Intelligence)
2. **Content Adaptation** — en tres niveles: Translation, Transcreation, Creation
3. **Content Distribution**
4. **Content Performance**

## Afirmaciones centrales (textual)

> "Native Reach is to multilingual content what Inbound Marketing was to digital content."

> Combina "**AI for efficiency**" y "**Human Intelligence for effectiveness**".

> Transforma el contenido multilingüe "from a cost center into a growth engine".
